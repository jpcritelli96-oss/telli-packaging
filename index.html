About Joe

I'm Joe Critelli, a Clemson University Packaging Science grad. I've spent about 8 years in CPG packaging at Mars, Kellanova (Kellogg's), and Home Market Foods, plus about 15 years in restaurants, so I know food and beverage from the kitchen to the shelf.

I started Telli Packaging Consulting to give growing brands the same packaging expertise the big companies have, without the cost of a full-time hire. Based in Massachusetts, serving New England and the East Coast.

Who it's for
